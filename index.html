A few helpful details

Frequently asked questions

How much do your cakes and treats cost?

Prices depend on the size, flavour, design, quantity and packaging. Send your requirements and budget through the order form and we will discuss a quote with you.

How far in advance should I order?

Please contact us as early as possible, especially for custom cakes, larger quantities and special dates. We will confirm whether your requested date is available.

Do you deliver?

We serve Abuja and Keffi. Share your delivery or collection area when ordering so we can confirm the available arrangement and any delivery fee.

Can I request a custom design or message?

Yes. Describe your theme, colours, preferred message and any reference idea in the order form. We will discuss what can be made for your order.

Is my order confirmed after I submit the form?

Not yet. Submitting the form opens WhatsApp with your request. Your order is confirmed only after Maymahs Treats replies and agrees on the details, price and arrangements.
